Case 01

Probabilistic decision support,
audited end to end

A natural-language interface to probabilistic risk assessment. The model is never asked to do arithmetic: it interprets the question, gathers evidence, and hands the problem to Bayesian, decision-theoretic and multi-criteria solvers. Every fact carries its source and a quality tier, every model returns uncertainty, and every recommendation can be traced back to the sentence it came from. Against five language models across four scenarios, composite decision quality improved by 8 to 89 percent, with the largest gains in attribution and traceability.
